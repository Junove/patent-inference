Drafting a patent is slow, error-prone, and sensitive

Scattered prior art

Manual search across separate databases misses related work described with different words.

Inconsistent structure

Section order and claim-numbering rules are easy to violate, and easy to miss on review.

Slow iteration

Redrafting one section usually means re-reading the whole document to stay consistent.

No protection yet

Not legally protected until filed and approved — drafts have to stay private the whole time.

Four pain points: prior art is scattered, structure rules are easy to get wrong, every edit costs a full re-read, and none of this is legally protected yet, so it all has to stay private. That last one shapes the whole design: everything runs locally, and nothing leaves the backend except what a draft actually needs.
